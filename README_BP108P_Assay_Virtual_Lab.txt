BP108P Pharmaceutical Analysis Practical — Assay Virtual Laboratory

Open:
1. Download and extract the ZIP package.
2. Double-click BP108P_Assay_Compounds_Virtual_Laboratory.html.
3. Open with Chrome or Microsoft Edge. No installation or internet connection is required.

Includes:
1. Ammonium chloride by acid-base titration
2. Ferrous sulphate by cerimetry
3. Copper sulphate by iodometry
4. Calcium gluconate by complexometry
5. Hydrogen peroxide by permanganometry
6. Sodium benzoate by non-aqueous titration
7. Sodium chloride by precipitation titration

Features:
Principle, reaction, titrant standardization concept, guided conceptual steps, sample/titre inputs, illustrative calculation, student record, Word-compatible DOC/PDF/TXT export, CSV log and quiz.

Important:
The calculator is educational and uses illustrative factors. It is not a validated assay calculator. Verify exact reaction stoichiometry, standard form/hydrate, titrant standardization, official assay factor, solvent, indicator, endpoint and blank correction against the current official pharmacopoeia and institution-approved practical manual before laboratory use. Follow all safety requirements.
